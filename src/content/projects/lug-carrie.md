## Building a Mobility Movement

Lug+Carrie came to us with a simple goal: get more families on bikes. To do this, we needed to move beyond the traditional "cyclist" demographic and speak to everyday commuters and parents who were tired of traffic and parking struggles.

### The Subscription Model

We helped highlight the ease of the subscription model—no huge upfront costs, full maintenance included, and the flexibility to swap bikes as families grow.

### Brand Voice

The brand needed to be approachable, fun, and Melbourne-centric. We developed a visual language and tone of voice that made the cargo bike transition feel like the smartest move a parent could make.
